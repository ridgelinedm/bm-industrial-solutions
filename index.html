Why plants consolidate with us

Four trades, one contractor, nobody to point at

When compressed air, hydraulics, rigging and piping are split across four vendors, every problem lands in the gap between them. We close the gap by covering all four.

One vendor for everything

The install, the piping and the service, all one crew

A compressor installation is really four jobs: setting the equipment, piping the distribution, connecting the utilities and commissioning the system.

Split those across separate contractors and you own the coordination risk. We take the whole scope, which means when something needs adjusting six months later, there is one number to call and no argument about whose scope it was.

Questions

Straight answers

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Holly Hill, South Carolina, and run regular field service across South Carolina, Georgia and North Carolina, including Charleston, North Charleston, Summerville, Columbia, Greenville, Spartanburg, Aiken, Sumter, Augusta, Savannah and the Charlotte area. See all service areas.

Do you really answer the phone at night?

Yes. The emergency line runs 24 hours a day, including weekends and holidays. You reach someone who can assess the problem and dispatch. Call 843-401-9670.

Which compressor brands do you service?

Gardner Denver, Sullair, Ingersoll Rand, Kaeser, Atlas Copco, CompAir/LeRoi, Quincy, Champion, Curtis and Saylor-Beall, plus most other platforms. We are independent, so we recommend what the application needs rather than what a dealer agreement requires.

Can you handle a whole project, or only service calls?

Both. We do emergency repairs, scheduled maintenance, and complete installation projects including rigging, equipment setting and the process piping that connects it. See mechanical contracting.

Do you work around production schedules?

Routinely. Most installation and changeover work happens on weekends, night shifts or planned shutdowns so the disruptive part fits the window you actually have.
