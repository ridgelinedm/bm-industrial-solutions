Questions

Before you call

Do we have to use you for all four services?

No. Plenty of customers use us only for compressed air. The four lines exist because they overlap constantly in practice, but you are not obligated to consolidate anything.

Are you a dealer for a particular manufacturer?

No, and that is deliberate. Being independent means we can recommend the equipment your application actually calls for instead of the line we are contracted to sell.

Can you quote a project from drawings?

We can start from drawings, but for anything involving existing plant we will want a site walk before committing to a number. Send what you have through the contact form and we will tell you what else we need.

How do you charge?

Service work is quoted on time and materials with the rate structure given up front. Projects and installations are quoted as a fixed scope after a site visit. Either way you know the basis before we start.
