Overview

Hydraulic Maintenance

Scheduled hydraulic maintenance is inexpensive compared with a pump replacement, and far cheaper than the production hours a failure takes with it.

We build the interval around duty cycle and environment, and we document what we find so you can see components trending toward trouble.

On a maintenance visit

  • Fluid service. Oil condition assessment, changes on interval, correct grade verified.
  • Filtration. Element changes, breather service, bypass indicator checks.
  • Hose inspection. Abrasion, routing, bend radius, age and fitting condition.
  • Pressure verification. Relief and system settings checked against specification.
  • Temperature and cooling. Cooler condition, fan operation, running temperature logged.
  • Leak and cylinder check. Rod seals, glands, fittings and mounting condition.

Questions

About hydraulic maintenance

How often should hydraulic oil be changed?

By condition wherever possible. Duty cycle, operating temperature and how well the reservoir is sealed against ingress matter far more than elapsed months. We will set a sensible starting interval and refine it.

Is hydraulic maintenance worth it on older equipment?

Particularly on older equipment. Components are harder to source and more expensive when they fail, which raises the value of preventing it.

Can hydraulic maintenance be combined with our compressor visits?

Yes, and it usually should be. One visit, one vendor, one invoice. That is the main reason customers use us for both.
