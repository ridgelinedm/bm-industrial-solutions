Overview

Air Compressor Systems

BM Industrial Solutions services every major compressor platform on the market: Gardner Denver, Sullair, Ingersoll Rand, Kaeser, Atlas Copco, CompAir/LeRoi, Quincy, Champion, Curtis and Saylor-Beall. Because we are not a factory dealer, we recommend what your application needs rather than what a distributor agreement requires.

Most of the calls we take are not really compressor problems. They are dryer problems, drain problems, filtration problems or piping problems that show up at the compressor. We diagnose the whole system, so the fix holds.

What we handle

  • Rotary screw service — oil and separator changes, air/oil filters, coolers, bearings, airend inspection and rebuild, VSD drive troubleshooting.
  • Reciprocating service — valve service, rings and gaskets, unloaders, pressure switches, belt drives and pump rebuilds.
  • Air treatment — refrigerated and desiccant dryer service, coalescing and particulate filtration, condensate drains and oil-water separators.
  • Controls and monitoring — pressure and load-hour troubleshooting, sequencing between multiple units, alarm and shutdown diagnosis.
  • System installation — sizing, foundations, electrical coordination, receiver tanks and complete air distribution piping.
  • Emergency response — 24/7 breakdown coverage, including temporary air where production cannot wait for a repair.

Right-sized systems

Sized for the plant, not the catalogue

An oversized compressor short-cycles, wastes energy and wears out early. An undersized one starves your tools and never unloads.

We measure actual demand before we quote anything — running pressure, pressure drop across the system, load and unload behaviour, and where your air is genuinely being consumed. Sometimes the answer is a smaller compressor and better piping. We will tell you when that is the case.

Whole-system diagnosis

We find the cause, not just the symptom

High discharge temperature, water in the lines, pressure that will not hold, a unit that trips on overload — these are symptoms with several possible causes. We work through the system methodically rather than swapping parts and hoping.

  • Water at the tool usually means a dryer or drain problem, not a compressor problem.
  • Pressure complaints at the far end of a plant are usually piping, not capacity.
  • Repeat overload trips often trace to the airend, cooler or supply voltage.

Questions

About air compressor systems

How quickly can you get to a compressor that is down?

We run 24/7 emergency coverage across South Carolina, Georgia and North Carolina. Response time depends on where you are and what the failure is — call 843-401-9670 and you will get a straight answer about arrival time rather than a queue position.

Do you only work on certain brands?

No. We service Gardner Denver, Sullair, Ingersoll Rand, Kaeser, Atlas Copco, CompAir/LeRoi, Quincy, Champion, Curtis and Saylor-Beall, plus most other platforms you are likely to have. We are independent, so we are not steering you toward one manufacturer.

How often should a rotary screw compressor be serviced?

Interval depends on run hours, ambient conditions and the lubricant in use, but most units need attention at set hour intervals for oil, separators and filters, with a fuller inspection annually. Dusty or hot environments shorten that considerably. We will set an interval based on your actual run hours rather than a generic sticker.

Can you install a new system as well as repair the one we have?

Yes — full installations including compressor, dryer, receiver, filtration and the distribution piping that connects it all. We handle the mechanical scope and coordinate with your electrician, or bring one in.

Is it worth repairing an older compressor?

Often, yes. A well-built reciprocating unit or an older rotary screw with a sound airend can be worth rebuilding. We will give you the honest comparison between repair cost, expected remaining life and the energy cost of continuing to run it.
