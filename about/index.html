About

Locally owned. Personally committed. Professionally built.

BM Industrial Solutions is an owner-operated industrial service company in Holly Hill, South Carolina. The person who quotes your job is the person accountable for it.

The owner

Bradley McLaughlin

A hands-on technician with over a decade of experience, still in the field on the jobs the company takes.

BM Industrial Solutions exists because industrial plants kept running into the same problem: the technician who knew the equipment worked for a company where you could never quite reach him, and the person you could reach had never seen your plant.

Being owner-operated collapses that. There is no dispatch layer between the customer and the work, and no incentive to sell equipment the application does not need.

How we work

Four commitments we actually keep

These are not aspirations on a wall. They are the reason customers consolidate work with us.

You get the owner, not a queue

The person quoting the work is the person doing it or supervising it. Decisions get made on your floor rather than routed through a branch office three counties away.

Diagnosis before parts

Swapping components until the symptom goes away is expensive and it does not hold. We work the system methodically and fix the cause, which is why we are not back next quarter for the same fault.

Independent of manufacturers

We are not a dealer for anybody. That means the recommendation you get is based on what your application needs, not on a distribution agreement we are trying to satisfy.

Written down, handed over

What was checked, what was changed, what the readings were and what is trending toward a problem. A record you can plan a budget against.
